Brainy Bee Schools Services Agreement

Draft date: August 1, 2026
Agreement version: Draft 0.1

This School Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Chi Cheung Lau, doing business as Brainy Bee ("Provider"), and the school, school district, or other educational agency identified in the Order Form ("School"). The Agreement becomes effective on the last signature date unless the Order Form states another date.

1. Agreement documents and priority

The Agreement consists of: (a) the Order Form; (b) this School Services Agreement; (c) the Brainy Bee Schools Data Privacy Addendum ("DPA"); and (d) any state-specific student data addendum signed by both parties.

If documents conflict, a signed state-specific addendum controls for the subject it addresses, then the DPA controls for privacy and security, then the Order Form, and then this Agreement.

2. Service

Brainy Bee Schools is a mobile educational service that provides school-authorized math practice, assignments, class controls, progress tracking, achievement features, and teacher reporting for basic arithmetic. Production school access is provided through Clever single sign-on and an organization license.

Provider grants School a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the subscription term to allow authorized teachers and students to use the Service for School's internal educational purposes.

3. School administration and authorized users

School will identify an authorized administrative contact outside the app. School controls which users and classes participate through its Clever configuration and the organization records maintained for the School. School will:

  • provide accurate organization and authorization information;
  • authorize only users associated with School and promptly remove access when no longer appropriate;
  • designate staff with legitimate educational interests to access student records;
  • use the Service and exported information only for lawful educational and school-administration purposes; and
  • notify Provider promptly of suspected unauthorized access or compromised credentials.

Provider does not delegate its own COPPA or privacy-law compliance responsibilities to School. School remains responsible for its own educational decisions, notices, records, and obligations under laws applicable to School.

4. Student privacy authorization

Before student personal information is collected, School confirms that its authorized decision-maker has reviewed the COPPA Direct Notice, Privacy Policy, and DPA and has approved the Service for the educational use described in the Order Form.

For US students under 13, School authorizes Provider to collect and process personal information only for the school-requested educational service and no unrelated commercial purpose. For FERPA-covered records disclosed under the school-official exception, the DPA establishes School's direct control over Provider's use and maintenance of those records.

5. Acceptable use

Authorized users may not:

  • attempt to access another school, class, user account, or record without authorization;
  • share credentials or permit a student to use staff access;
  • upload malicious code, unlawful content, or content that violates another person's rights;
  • interfere with the Service or bypass security or class controls;
  • use the Service or Student Data for advertising, marketing, surveillance, punishment unrelated to education, or another unauthorized purpose; or
  • export or redisclose Student Data outside School-approved systems except as permitted by law.

6. Fees and license

Fees, license limits, taxes, payment dates, and subscription dates are specified in the Order Form. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, fees are quoted in Canadian dollars (CAD) and are non-refundable except where this Agreement expressly provides otherwise or applicable law requires.

School's license may specify unlimited or capped student seats. Teacher accounts do not consume student seats unless the Order Form states otherwise. Provider will not ask students to purchase access or display consumer subscription offers in the Schools Service.

7. Ownership

School retains all rights it has in Student Data and School-provided content. Provider receives only the limited right to process that information to provide, secure, support, and maintain the school-requested Service as described in the DPA.

Provider retains all rights in the Brainy Bee software, educational content, design, trademarks, and documentation, excluding School-provided content and Student Data. Feedback may be used to improve the Service only if it does not contain Student Data or confidential School information.

8. Privacy, security, and subprocessors

Provider will process Student Data according to the DPA, Privacy Policy, COPPA Direct Notice, published retention policy, and applicable law. Provider will maintain a written information security program and will use subprocessors only as described in the DPA.

9. Support and service changes

Support is available through paulofrancis.software@gmail.com. Provider will use reasonable efforts to maintain the Service but does not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Scheduled maintenance and events outside Provider's reasonable control may affect access.

Provider may improve the Service during the term but will not materially reduce privacy protections or materially expand Student Data collection or use without advance notice and any authorization required by law.

10. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's non-public business, technical, and personal information using at least reasonable care and will use it only to perform this Agreement. These restrictions do not apply to information that is public through no breach, already lawfully known without restriction, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

If disclosure is legally required, the receiving party will, where permitted, give prompt notice and disclose only what is required.

11. Term and termination

The term is stated in the Order Form. Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within 30 days after written notice. A party may terminate immediately when necessary to prevent ongoing unlawful processing, a material security threat, or serious harm.

At termination, authorized user access ends. Provider will make School Data available for export for 30 days and then delete it according to the DPA and published retention schedule, unless School requests earlier deletion or law requires retention.

12. Warranties and disclaimer

Each signer represents that they have authority to bind the identified party. Provider warrants that it will provide the Service substantially as described and process Student Data as stated in the DPA.

Brainy Bee Schools supplements classroom instruction and does not guarantee a particular academic outcome. Except for express commitments in this Agreement and to the maximum extent permitted by law, the Service is provided "as available" and implied warranties are disclaimed.

13. Liability and indemnity

13.1 Excluded damages

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party will be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages, or for lost profits or revenues, arising from this Agreement, even if advised that such damages were possible. This exclusion does not prevent recovery of direct and reasonable costs arising from a breach of confidentiality, a Security Incident, a violation of the DPA, or a third-party claim covered by this section.

13.2 General liability cap

Except for Enhanced-Cap Claims and Uncapped Claims defined below, each party's total aggregate liability arising from this Agreement will not exceed the greater of: (a) the fees paid or payable by School for the Service during the 12 months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim; or (b) CAD 1,000.

13.3 Enhanced-Cap and Uncapped Claims

Each party's total aggregate liability for breach of confidentiality, its privacy or information-security obligations, or its indemnification obligations (collectively, "Enhanced-Cap Claims") will not exceed the greater of: (a) two times the fees paid or payable during the 12 months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim; or (b) CAD 10,000.

No limitation or exclusion in this Agreement applies to fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, infringement or misappropriation of the other party's intellectual property, payment obligations, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited (collectively, "Uncapped Claims").

13.4 Provider indemnity

Provider will defend School from a third-party claim alleging that School's authorized use of the unmodified Service infringes a Canadian or United States patent, copyright, or trademark, or arising directly from Provider's material breach of the DPA. Provider will indemnify School for damages and reasonable costs finally awarded by a court or agreed in a settlement approved by Provider.

Provider has no obligation for a claim caused by School-provided content, unauthorized modification or use, combination with an item not supplied or approved by Provider, or continued use after Provider offers a reasonable non-infringing replacement. For an infringement claim, Provider may obtain the right to continue use, modify or replace the affected Service, or terminate the affected Service and refund prepaid fees covering the unused terminated period.

13.5 School responsibility and claim process

To the extent permitted by law, School will be responsible for third-party claims arising from School-provided content or School's material breach of the acceptable-use restrictions. Nothing in this Agreement requires a public educational entity to provide indemnification when doing so is prohibited by law.

An indemnified party must give prompt written notice, allow the indemnifying party to control the defence and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. No settlement may admit fault by, impose non-monetary obligations on, or fail to fully release the indemnified party without its written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

Counsel review required. The limits above are a proposed starting position, not a recommendation that they are adequate for Provider's insurance or acceptable under a School's procurement rules. Qualified counsel should review this section before either party signs.

14. General terms

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger, reorganization, or sale of substantially all relevant assets that accepts the Agreement in writing and is legally permitted to receive the data.

Notices under this Agreement must be sent to the contacts in the Order Form and are effective when confirmed by email or delivery service. The parties are independent contractors. If a provision is unenforceable, it will be narrowed as needed and the remaining provisions will continue.

Before filing a claim, the parties will attempt in good faith for at least 30 days to resolve the dispute through representatives authorized to settle it. Either party may seek urgent injunctive or protective relief without waiting 30 days.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Quebec and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Subject to mandatory law, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in the Judicial District of Longueuil, Quebec. A mutually signed Order Form or required public-school addendum may specify different governing law, venue, or dispute procedures.
