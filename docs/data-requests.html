School and Student Data Request Procedure

Draft date: August 1, 2026
Request email: paulofrancis.software@gmail.com

Schools may request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion of information associated with their users. Parents, guardians, and eligible students should normally begin with their school so the school can verify identity and coordinate all education records.

1. Available requests

  • Description: categories and purposes of information processed for the School or student.
  • Access: review of identifiable information associated with an authorized user.
  • Correction: correction of inaccurate account, roster, or educational information.
  • Export: a structured, commonly used copy of requested information.
  • Restriction: an end to further use or collection for a user or School.
  • Deletion: deletion of a student's records or offboarding of an entire School organization.

2. Requests from a school or district

An authorized School representative should email the request address with the subject `Brainy Bee Schools Data Request` and provide:

  • School or district legal name;
  • requester's name, title, school email, and phone number;
  • type of request and requested completion date;
  • organization, school, class, school year, and affected user identifiers needed to locate records; and
  • whether collection or access should be suspended while the request is processed.

Do not include passwords, Clever tokens, student answer reports, or unnecessary student records in ordinary email. Provider will establish an appropriate secure exchange method when an export or sensitive evidence is required.

3. Requests from a parent, guardian, or eligible student

Begin with the School's privacy, records, or administrative contact. The School can verify identity, determine educational-record rights, and request action from Brainy Bee.

A requester may also contact Brainy Bee directly. Provide the School name, student's display name or School-provided identifier, relationship to the student, requested action, and a way to contact you. Do not email copies of identification until Brainy Bee provides a secure method and explains why verification is necessary.

Brainy Bee may coordinate with the School before changing or disclosing an education record. This protects students and avoids disclosing information to an unauthorized person.

4. Verification

Provider verifies only what is reasonably necessary for the request. Verification may include confirming the request through a known School administrative contact, matching a School-domain email, conducting a callback using existing contact records, or requesting School confirmation of a parent's authority.

If identity or authority cannot be verified, Provider will explain what additional information is needed or will deny the request without disclosing whether a student record exists.

5. Processing and timing

  1. Acknowledge receipt and assign a request reference.
  2. Verify identity, authority, organization, student scope, and requested action.
  3. Search relevant authentication, roster, class, assignment, result, achievement, preference, storage, and support systems.
  4. Coordinate with School on correction, export format, educational-record effects, and any lawful retention exception.
  5. When authorized, place a reversible stop-collection hold on the affected student or School while the request is reviewed. The hold restricts application access and prevents new authenticated writes; it does not itself delete records.
  6. For a correction, update the identified Brainy Bee records and authentication metadata. If Clever supplied the inaccurate value, coordinate correction of the source roster with the School so a later sync does not restore it. A shared identity requires coordination with every affected School.
  7. For an export or deletion, use the access-restricted administrative workflow to inventory the requested scope, preserve the authorized export privately, execute only after project and scope confirmation, and verify scoped removal.
  8. Complete the request within the time required by applicable law. The operational target is 30 days for ordinary access, correction, and export requests.
  9. For deletion, remove production data within 60 days after authorization and allow inaccessible Firestore point-in-time recovery versions to expire through the configured seven-day retention period.
  10. Provide completion confirmation without reproducing deleted Student Data.

6. Export format and delivery

Exports may be provided in CSV, JSON, PDF, or another structured commonly used format appropriate to the records. Brainy Bee prepares a disclosure copy limited to the authorized records; the internal administrative before-state archive is not delivered unchanged.

Sensitive exports are delivered according to the Secure Export Delivery Procedure, normally through a School-provided authenticated portal restricted to the verified recipient. Student Data is not sent as an ordinary email attachment, stored in this public repository, or shared through a public or "anyone with the link" URL.

7. Denial, limitation, or extension

A request may be limited when the requester is not authorized, disclosure would expose another person's information, the record is not maintained by Brainy Bee, the request conflicts with law, or a narrow documented retention exception applies. Provider will explain the reason and available complaint or appeal path unless prohibited by law.

8. Request record

Provider keeps a limited record of the request type, verification method, scope, decisions, completion date, and confirmation. The request record should not contain copies of the Student Data that was exported or deleted.
