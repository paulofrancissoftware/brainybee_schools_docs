Brainy Bee Schools Data Privacy Addendum

Draft date: August 1, 2026
Addendum version: Draft 0.1

This Data Privacy Addendum ("DPA") is incorporated into the Brainy Bee Schools Services Agreement between Chi Cheung Lau, doing business as Brainy Bee ("Provider"), and the educational agency identified in the applicable Order Form ("School").

1. Definitions

  • Applicable Privacy Law means privacy, children's privacy, student privacy, breach-notification, and education-record laws applicable to a party's activities under the Agreement, including COPPA and, where applicable, FERPA and state student privacy law.
  • COPPA means the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and Rule, 15 U.S.C. Sections 6501-6506 and 16 C.F.R. Part 312, as amended.
  • FERPA means the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act and its regulations, 20 U.S.C. Section 1232g and 34 C.F.R. Part 99.
  • Personal Information includes personal information, personal data, and personally identifiable information protected by Applicable Privacy Law.
  • Security Incident means confirmed unauthorized access to, acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of Student Data, excluding unsuccessful attempts that do not compromise Student Data.
  • Student Data means Personal Information maintained by Provider that is directly related to an identifiable current or former School student and is received from School or Clever, or created through the student's school-authorized use of the Service.
  • Subprocessor means a service provider engaged by Provider to process Student Data in order to deliver the Service.

2. Roles and instructions

School determines the educational purpose for using Brainy Bee Schools and controls which authorized users participate. Provider processes Student Data only to provide, secure, support, and maintain the Service described in the Agreement and documented instructions consistent with that purpose.

School's instructions include the Agreement, this DPA, the Order Form, school-configured class controls, authorized support requests, and legally valid requests to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete Student Data.

3. FERPA school-official commitments

When School discloses education records under FERPA's school-official exception, Provider agrees that it:

  • performs an institutional service or function for which School would otherwise use employees;
  • is under School's direct control with respect to the use and maintenance of education records as established by this DPA;
  • will use education-record information only for the purposes for which School disclosed it;
  • will not redisclose personally identifiable information from education records except to authorized personnel and Subprocessors with a need to provide the Service, or as otherwise permitted by FERPA and this DPA;
  • will allow School to exercise required access, correction, record-management, and disclosure-control rights; and
  • will return or delete education-record information when no longer needed for the school-authorized purpose, subject to Section 11.

School will determine whether its use satisfies the criteria in its annual FERPA notice for a school official with a legitimate educational interest.

4. COPPA commitments and school authorization

Before collecting Personal Information online from students under 13 based on school authorization, Provider will give School the COPPA Direct Notice describing collection, use, disclosure, retention, and rights.

School authorization applies only to the use and benefit of School in the educational context described in the Order Form. Provider remains responsible for its compliance with COPPA and will not use school-authorized children's information for an unrelated commercial purpose.

Upon a verified request, Provider will give School or a legally authorized parent a description of information collected from a child, an opportunity to review it, the right to request deletion, and a way to prevent further use or collection. If a parent contacts Provider directly, Provider may coordinate with School to verify identity and authority and to protect education records.

5. Data minimization and prohibited uses

Provider will collect, use, and retain only Student Data reasonably necessary for the documented school-authorized educational purpose. Provider will not:

  • sell, rent, or otherwise exchange Student Data for money or another commercial benefit;
  • use Student Data for targeted advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or marketing to a student or parent;
  • build a commercial profile of a student except a school-authorized educational progress profile used only to provide the Service;
  • use Student Data to develop an unrelated product or service;
  • require a student to disclose more Personal Information than is reasonably necessary to participate;
  • permit students to make Personal Information publicly available through the Service; or
  • retain Student Data indefinitely.

Provider may use aggregated or de-identified information only when it cannot reasonably identify a student or School, Provider does not attempt re-identification, and the use is limited to securing, supporting, measuring, or improving the school-requested Service. Provider will not disclose such information in a manner that identifies a student or School.

6. Access and confidentiality

Provider will limit access to Student Data to personnel and Subprocessors who need access to provide or secure the Service and who are bound by confidentiality and data-protection duties. Provider will review administrative access periodically and remove it when no longer needed.

School will limit teacher and staff access to users with legitimate educational interests and will protect exported Student Data according to School policy and applicable law.

7. Information security program

Provider will establish, implement, and maintain a written information security program appropriate to the sensitivity of Student Data; Provider's size, complexity, and activities; and the nature and scope of the Service. At minimum, Provider will:

  • designate an employee or responsible individual to coordinate the security program;
  • identify reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks and reassess them at least annually and after material changes;
  • design and maintain safeguards to control identified risks;
  • use authentication, authorization, least privilege, encryption in transit, secure configuration, and protected administrative credentials;
  • test or monitor safeguards and address identified weaknesses;
  • review Subprocessor privacy and security capabilities and obtain written assurances where required;
  • maintain an incident-response plan and securely dispose of data; and
  • evaluate and update the security program at least annually.

The public Security Overview describes current safeguards without disclosing sensitive security details.

8. Security incidents

Provider will investigate a suspected Security Incident and take reasonable measures to contain, mitigate, and prevent recurrence. Provider will notify School without unreasonable delay and, unless law enforcement or applicable law requires otherwise, no later than 72 hours after confirming a Security Incident affecting School's Student Data.

The notice will include, as information becomes reasonably available: the nature and approximate timing of the incident; affected data categories and users; known or likely consequences; containment and remediation steps; a contact for follow-up; and information reasonably needed for School's legal assessment and notices.

Provider will not notify students, parents, regulators, or the public on School's behalf without coordination, unless Provider has an independent legal duty to do so. Each party remains responsible for notices legally assigned to it.

9. Subprocessors

School generally authorizes the Subprocessors listed at Subprocessors as of the effective date. Provider will:

  • use a Subprocessor only for a purpose necessary to provide or secure the Service;
  • require privacy, confidentiality, security, deletion, and use restrictions appropriate to the Student Data processed;
  • remain responsible for the Subprocessor's processing to the extent required by the Agreement and law; and
  • give School at least 30 days' notice before adding a Subprocessor that will materially process Student Data, when practicable.

School may raise a reasonable, documented student-privacy objection during the notice period. The parties will work in good faith on an alternative. If none is reasonably available, School may stop using the affected feature or terminate the affected Service without penalty.

10. Access, correction, export, and restriction

Provider will provide reasonable assistance so School can respond to verified requests involving Student Data. School may request access, correction, a structured commonly used export, restriction of further collection or use, or deletion by following the Data Request Procedure.

Provider will not materially alter an education record at the request of a student or parent without School's authorization unless applicable law requires direct action. Provider may correct technical account information needed to secure the Service and will inform School when appropriate.

11. Retention, return, and deletion

Provider will follow the published Data Retention and Deletion Policy. Unless School gives different lawful instructions:

  • Student Data remains available during the active subscription for current instruction and School-requested historical reporting;
  • School has 30 days after termination to request an export;
  • Provider deletes Student Data from production systems within 60 days after termination or an authorized deletion request; and
  • residual backup copies expire or are rendered inaccessible within 90 days after production deletion.

Provider may retain a narrowly limited record if required by law, necessary to document compliance, resolve a dispute, or investigate security abuse. Such retained information remains protected, is isolated from ordinary product use, and is deleted when the reason ends.

Upon request, Provider will give School written confirmation of completed deletion. The confirmation will identify the scope and completion date without reproducing Student Data.

12. Legal requests and redisclosure

If Provider receives legal process seeking School's Student Data, Provider will, where legally permitted, notify School promptly and give School a reasonable opportunity to seek protection. Provider will disclose only information legally required.

Provider will not voluntarily redisclose Student Data to law enforcement, government, or another third party except at School's documented direction or when reasonably necessary to address an imminent safety threat or protect the Service, and only as permitted by applicable law.

13. Cross-border processing

Provider operates from Quebec, Canada, and uses providers that may process information outside Quebec or outside a student's country. Provider will make required cross-border disclosures, conduct privacy impact assessments where applicable, and use written agreements intended to provide appropriate protection.

14. Records and assurance

Upon reasonable written request no more than once annually, Provider will supply information reasonably sufficient for School to evaluate Provider's compliance with this DPA, such as current policies, Subprocessor information, security questionnaire responses, and relevant test summaries. Provider is not required to disclose source code, credentials, information about another customer, or details that would weaken security.

If law or a material Security Incident reasonably requires additional review, the parties will agree on a proportionate process that protects confidentiality and minimizes disruption.

15. State and local requirements

School may provide a required state or district student-data addendum for review. A mutually signed addendum controls over this DPA only for the requirements it specifically addresses. Provider does not represent through this general template that it satisfies every state procurement or student privacy form without review.

16. Duration and survival

This DPA remains effective while Provider processes Student Data for School. Confidentiality, use restrictions, security, incident cooperation, and deletion duties survive termination for as long as Provider retains Student Data.
