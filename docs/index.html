School review templates

These templates are generic and contain no school-specific terms. They remain drafts until legal review and must be customized and privately executed with each school. Ask the district for its required DPA or state addendum and authorized signatory. The agreement/Order Form and DPA contain the contractual terms; the COPPA notice has a receipt acknowledgment, and a privacy policy is not normally separately signed.

School Services AgreementDraft license and service terms for a school or district. Data Privacy AddendumDraft FERPA, COPPA, security, retention, and student-data commitments. Parent/Guardian NoticeA draft template schools may adapt and distribute to families.
