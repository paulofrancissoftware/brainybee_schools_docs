Brainy Bee Schools Privacy Center

Public policies effective August 15, 2026, plus school review templates

Brainy Bee Schools is a school-authorized math practice service for teachers and students. The published policies below explain what data the service processes, how it is protected, and how schools and families can exercise their rights. Contract and notice templates are provided separately for school and legal review.

Published policies and notices

Privacy PolicyComplete public notice for schools, families, teachers, and students. COPPA Direct NoticeSchool and parent notice describing collection, use, disclosure, and rights. Retention and DeletionImplemented lifecycle and offboarding schedule for school data. Security OverviewPublic description of safeguards and shared responsibilities. SubprocessorsProviders used to authenticate, host, and deliver the service. Data RequestsHow schools and families request access, correction, export, or deletion.

School review templates

These templates are generic and contain no school-specific terms. They remain drafts until legal review and must be customized and privately executed with each school.

School Services AgreementDraft license and service terms for a school or district. Data Privacy AddendumDraft FERPA, COPPA, security, retention, and student-data commitments. Parent/Guardian NoticeA draft template schools may adapt and distribute to families.
