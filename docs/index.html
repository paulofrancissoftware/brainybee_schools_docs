Brainy Bee Schools

Privacy, student data, security, and school contracting documents

Brainy Bee Schools is a school-authorized math practice service for teachers and students. These documents explain what data the service processes, how schools can exercise control, and the terms proposed for school use.
Privacy PolicyComplete public notice for schools, families, teachers, and students. COPPA Direct NoticeSchool and parent notice describing collection, use, disclosure, and rights. Security OverviewPublic description of safeguards and shared responsibilities. SubprocessorsProviders used to authenticate, host, and deliver the service. Information Security ProgramWritten administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Risk AssessmentInitial privacy and security risks, controls, and residual ratings. Quebec Privacy Impact AssessmentInitial assessment of processing outside Quebec.
