Quebec Cross-Border Privacy Impact Assessment

Organization: Chi Cheung Lau, doing business as Brainy Bee
Person responsible: Chi Cheung Lau, Owner and Privacy Officer
Assessment date: August 2, 2026
Legal reference: Section 17, Quebec Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector

Brainy Bee operates from Quebec and uses US-based cloud and school identity services. This assessment considers sensitivity, purpose, safeguards including contracts, and the legal regimes in the receiving locations. It records an initial operational decision; Quebec counsel must validate the analysis and applicable written agreements before the legal documents become final.

1. Project and purpose

Brainy Bee Schools provides school-authorized mental-math practice, assignments, class controls, and progress reporting. Cross-border processing is necessary because the production Firestore database is in the nam5 United States multi-region, Cloud Functions and Scheduler are in us-central1, and Clever operates its school identity/roster services in the United States and other locations identified in its terms.

2. People and information

Affected people include students, teachers, and limited School contacts. Information may include Clever and Firebase identifiers, name and email when provided, role, school/district, grade, class membership, assignments, answers, scores, progress, preferences, authorization state, IP/request metadata, and security logs.

Student identity, education activity, and performance are treated as sensitive because they concern children and may be education records. The Service does not intentionally collect payment-card information, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, health data, camera or microphone content, public posts, or advertising identifiers.

3. Necessity and proportionality

  • Processing is limited to school authorization, access control, math practice, teacher reporting, support, security, and legal obligations.
  • Production sign-in is Clever-only. The app does not use advertising, cross-context tracking, external QR generation, remote fonts, or optional analytics SDKs.
  • School and class access is role-limited. Students cannot browse an unrestricted directory or publish information publicly.
  • Data is retained under a written schedule and can be inventoried, exported, corrected, held, or deleted through guarded administrative tools.

5. Legal environment considered

Information processed in the United States may be subject to federal and state privacy, education, law-enforcement, court-order, and national-security authorities. US protections differ from Quebec law and may not provide identical rights or remedies. For participating US public schools, COPPA, FERPA, PPRA, state student-privacy law, School contracts, and provider terms may also protect the information. Contractual and technical controls reduce but cannot eliminate foreign-government or legal-process risk.

6. Safeguards

  • Written purpose limits, no sale/advertising restrictions, confidentiality, security, incident, deletion, and subprocessor terms in the School DPA and provider terms.
  • HTTPS/TLS, Clever/Firebase authentication, organization/class authorization, least-privilege runtime IAM, Secret Manager, and restricted production administration.
  • Five-minute single-use OAuth state, scheduled cleanup, 30-day ordinary logs, 400-day required audit logs, and seven-day Firestore recovery versions.
  • Mobile backup exclusion, no Firebase Storage bucket, no advertising identifier permission, and no optional tracking SDK.
  • Verified data-request workflow, stop-collection holds, scoped deletion, and public notice of providers and cross-border processing.

8. Decision and conditions

The Privacy Officer's preliminary assessment is that the limited processing may provide adequate protection for the pilot when all described safeguards and written agreements apply. Authorization is conditional on:

  • qualified Quebec counsel confirming the Section 17 analysis and written contractual protection;
  • preserving private evidence of the applicable Google and Clever terms accepted for the production accounts;
  • not adding a new material provider, purpose, sensitive data category, or location without updating this assessment first; and
  • completing a French-language and Quebec-school-specific review before serving students through a Quebec school.
