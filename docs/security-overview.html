Brainy Bee Schools Security Overview

Current adopted policy: Version 1.0, effective August 15, 2026
Proposed revision prepared: November 17, 2026
Proposed revision: 1.1 draft
Security contact: paulofrancis.software@gmail.com

Brainy Bee Schools uses layered technical and administrative safeguards intended to protect school and student information. This public overview avoids operational details that could weaken security. More detailed responses may be provided to a verified School under appropriate confidentiality terms.

1. Security governance

  • A designated coordinator is responsible for the written information security program.
  • Risks to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of student information are assessed at least annually and after material system changes.
  • Policies cover access, credentials, service providers, secure development, retention, deletion, incidents, and recovery.
  • Security findings are tracked to resolution according to risk.

2. Identity and access

  • Production school users authenticate through Clever and receive a Firebase session tied to the verified Clever identity. A separately controlled demonstration entry, where enabled, also uses Clever synthetic accounts; it is not consumer email/password enrollment.
  • Access is evaluated using organization membership, role, school license status, class membership, and class policy.
  • Students cannot grant themselves teacher permissions, edit organization licenses, or browse the full school directory.
  • Authorized teachers receive access only to assigned or owned classes and associated educational records.
  • Administrative access to production services is restricted to personnel with a documented need and is reviewed periodically.

3. Data protection

  • Network communication with Firebase, Clever, and published legal resources uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Student Data is stored in Firebase services with access controlled by authenticated identities, application authorization checks, and Firestore security rules.
  • Firebase client API keys identify the configured project; they are not a substitute for authenticated authorization and do not independently grant access to Student Data. Release checks must verify applicable API, app-identity, and signing-certificate restrictions against the actual deployed settings. This proposed overview does not attest that every such restriction has been verified for every current binary or distribution path.
  • Secrets and privileged service-account credentials must be stored outside source control and rotated when exposure is suspected.
  • Local app data is isolated within the mobile app's storage, and app-managed files are excluded from Apple and Android cloud backup and device transfer.
  • Student information is not used for advertising, and the Android Schools manifest removes advertising-ID permissions.

4. Application safeguards

  • Teacher and student interfaces expose different functionality based on verified school role.
  • Class policies restrict student access to assignments, practice modes, results, settings, reminders, and customization features.
  • Students cannot use the current Service to publish a public profile, public message, or unrestricted free-text post.
  • OAuth state expires after five minutes, is single-use, is deleted after redemption, and has a scheduled cleanup fallback.
  • Input size and document identifier checks are applied to school administration and Firebase operations.

Initial school-authorized roster import and recurring/background Clever synchronization are distinct from a student's interactive sign-in. School authorization and the approved roster-sharing scope must be established before processing real Student Data. Certification is not a substitute for school authorization, privacy review, or release-specific security verification.

5. Service providers

Provider reviews the purpose and data access of services that process Student Data and seeks written privacy and security assurances appropriate to their role. The current providers are listed on the Subprocessor List.

New providers that materially process Student Data are evaluated before use and communicated to Schools according to the Data Privacy Addendum.

6. Logging and monitoring

Authentication, cloud function, application error, and administrative events may be logged to diagnose failures and investigate misuse. Ordinary logs use 30-day rolling retention; Google-required audit logs use a locked 400-day retention period. Logs are access-restricted, and student answers and rosters are not intentionally copied into ordinary diagnostic messages.

7. Security incidents

Provider maintains a written incident-response process to validate, contain, investigate, remediate, document, and learn from suspected incidents. Affected Schools are notified without unreasonable delay and, under an executed DPA, no later than 72 hours after confirmation unless law enforcement or applicable law requires otherwise.

To report a suspected vulnerability or incident, email paulofrancis.software@gmail.com with "Brainy Bee Schools Security" in the subject. Do not include student records, credentials, or exploit details in an unencrypted initial email.

8. Retention, deletion, and recovery

Student Data is retained only while needed for the authorized educational purpose and is deleted according to the Data Retention and Deletion Policy. Firestore point-in-time recovery supports restoration from the previous seven days; recovery versions then expire automatically. No longer-lived managed Firestore backups are enabled. Database deletion protection guards against accidental database removal.

For an authorized stop-collection request, Brainy Bee can suspend the affected organization or student, revoke active sessions for exclusive accounts, and prevent application writes while the request is reviewed. Releasing a hold requires the identifier of the request that created it.

9. School responsibilities

  • Approve the Service through an authorized school or district process.
  • Configure Clever sharing and remove users who no longer require access.
  • Limit staff access to legitimate educational interests.
  • Protect devices and accounts, use available multi-factor authentication for administrative systems, and avoid shared staff credentials.
  • Store exports only in School-approved systems and report suspected misuse promptly.

10. Current assurance status

Brainy Bee Schools does not currently claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, or COPPA Safe Harbor certification. A claim of legal compliance or certification will be made only when supported by the applicable review or approval.

Historical evidence: production Firebase rules, runtime identity, credential storage, backup exclusions, logging configuration, database protection, deletion tooling, OAuth cleanup, risk assessment, and incident-response documentation were reviewed or tested on August 2, 2026. That historical review does not verify every current release or configuration. Current operational evidence and the final artifacts must be reconciled before this proposed revision is adopted.
