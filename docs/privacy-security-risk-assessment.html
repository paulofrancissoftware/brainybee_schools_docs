Privacy and Security Risk Assessment

Assessor and owner: Chi Cheung Lau, Owner and Privacy Officer
Assessment date: August 2, 2026
Scope: Brainy Bee Schools mobile apps, Firebase/Google Cloud backend, Clever integration, source repositories, and manual administration

The assessment considered reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks to children's and school information. The current release may proceed to a limited school pilot with the controls below, subject to counsel review, school authorization, store disclosure completion, and continued monitoring.

1. Method

Likelihood and impact are scored from 1 (low) to 5 (very high). Residual score is likelihood multiplied by impact after controls: 1-4 low, 5-9 moderate, 10-15 high, and 16-25 critical. A high or critical residual risk blocks production unless the Privacy Officer documents additional mitigation and acceptance.

2. Data sensitivity and exposure

Student identity, class membership, answers, scores, progress, and teacher assignments are sensitive because they concern children and may be education records. The Service does not intentionally collect payment cards, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, health data, camera or microphone content, public posts, or advertising identifiers. Production processing is concentrated in Clever and Google Firebase/Cloud in the United States.

4. Required continuing actions

  • Repeat the successfully completed August 2, 2026 synthetic point-in-time recovery exercise at least annually. Complete a School-approved synthetic export-transfer exercise before that workflow is used with real Student Data.
  • Review Google and Clever terms, assurance materials, and processing locations annually and after notice of change.
  • Review App Store and Google Play privacy disclosures against each release binary.
  • Obtain qualified US and Quebec legal review before removing legal draft notices or signing the pilot documents.
  • Identify an emergency technical contact and evaluate cyber/privacy insurance before a paid or materially larger deployment.

5. Decision

No residual technical risk is rated high or critical after the implemented controls. A limited, free pilot may proceed after the launch gates in the Publication Readiness Checklist are completed. This decision must be revisited after a material architecture change, a security event, a new subprocessor, or no later than August 2, 2027.
