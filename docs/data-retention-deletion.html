Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Draft date: August 1, 2026
Policy owner: Chi Cheung Lau, Owner and Privacy Officer
Review frequency: At least annually and after material product changes

Brainy Bee Schools retains personal information only while it is reasonably necessary for a specific school-authorized educational, security, support, or legal purpose. Children's personal information is not retained indefinitely.

1. Retention principles

  • Collect the minimum information reasonably necessary for the school-authorized activity.
  • Assign every category a purpose, owner, location, and deletion trigger.
  • Review active need at school offboarding and at least annually.
  • Delete securely when the purpose ends, including derived indexes and accessible copies.
  • Keep legal or security exceptions narrow, documented, access-restricted, and time-limited.
  • Do not use retained information for a new incompatible purpose.

3. School offboarding

  1. Verify the School representative's identity and authority.
  2. Record the termination date, affected organization, requested scope, and legal exceptions without copying Student Data into the request record.
  3. Disable new student sign-in and collection at the end of the subscription or on the authorized date.
  4. Provide a structured export when requested. The normal export window is 30 days after termination.
  5. Delete affected production data no later than 60 days after termination or the authorized deletion request.
  6. Allow residual encrypted backup copies to expire through rotation no later than 90 days after production deletion.
  7. Send written completion confirmation identifying scope and dates, but not reproducing Student Data.

4. Individual deletion

School may request deletion for one student while retaining other School records. Provider will identify account, roster, assignment membership, results, progress, preferences, room membership, and directly identifying references associated with that student and delete or de-identify them as authorized.

Where deletion would affect another student's record or a School record that must remain intact, Provider will remove the requesting student's direct identifiers and consult School about an appropriate resolution.

5. Legal and security exceptions

A retention exception must identify the record, reason, approving person, access restriction, and review or expiration date. Examples may include a preservation order, an unresolved payment dispute that does not require Student Data, or evidence needed to investigate a confirmed security incident.

Excepted data may not be used for ordinary product activity, marketing, profiling, or another incompatible purpose. It must be deleted when the exception ends.

6. Secure deletion

Deletion methods are appropriate to the storage system and may include document deletion, account deletion, encryption-key destruction, secure file deletion, storage-object deletion, database TTL, and backup rotation. Provider verifies deletion using system records or targeted checks and retains a confirmation record that does not contain the deleted Student Data.

7. Requests

Schools and verified families may submit retention or deletion questions using the Data Request Procedure or by emailing paulofrancis.software@gmail.com.

Manual Firebase export, correction, reversible access holds, scoped deletion, and post-operation verification are emulator-tested. Production access rules and Clever sign-in enforce active access status. The timing in this draft remains a proposed operational commitment until Clever cleanup, device and provider backup behavior, Cloud Logging retention, support records, and production administration are confirmed.
