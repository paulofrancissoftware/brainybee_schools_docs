[SCHOOL OR DISTRICT NAME] plans to use Brainy Bee Schools to support mathematics instruction for [CLASSES OR GRADE LEVELS] during [SCHOOL YEAR OR DATE RANGE].

What Brainy Bee Schools does

Brainy Bee Schools gives students school-selected arithmetic practice and assignments. It records results so students can receive feedback and authorized teachers can review progress. Teachers can control available operations, levels, assignments, reminders, avatar editing, and other classroom options.

Competition assignments may include a leaderboard visible to students enrolled in the assigned class. Students see their own entry as "You" and see classmates only under generic labels such as "Competitor 1," together with ranks and performance information. Teachers can see student names with results for educational review.

Information involved

Depending on the information our school shares through Clever and the features we use, Brainy Bee Schools may process:

  • the student's display name, school account identifiers, grade, school, and class membership;
  • assignments, answers, scores, question-level results, time spent, progress, and achievement badges;
  • avatar and app preferences; and
  • limited device, sign-in, diagnostic, and security information needed to run and protect the service.

The Schools service does not show advertising, sell student information, use student information for targeted advertising, or provide public student profiles or public chat.

Why the information is used

The information is used only to provide the school-authorized educational service: signing students in, placing them in the correct classes, delivering practice and assignments, calculating progress, giving teacher feedback, applying classroom controls, supporting users, and securing the service.

Service providers

Brainy Bee Schools uses Clever for school sign-in and roster information and Google Firebase and Google Cloud for account authentication, database, cloud functions, scheduling, logging, and technical infrastructure. Firebase Storage is not used by the production Schools Service. The current provider list is available at Subprocessors.

Your rights and choices

You may contact the school to ask what information is maintained about your child, request access or correction, request a copy, request deletion, or ask the school to stop further use or collection through Brainy Bee Schools. Ending collection or deleting required account information may mean your child can no longer use the service.

Please send requests to [SCHOOL PRIVACY OR RECORDS CONTACT]. The school will verify your identity and coordinate with Brainy Bee when needed. You may also contact the Brainy Bee Privacy Officer at paulofrancis.software@gmail.com.
