Brainy Bee Schools Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary. Brainy Bee Schools uses school account, class, assignment, and math-practice information only to provide and secure the educational service requested by a school or district. We do not sell student information, show advertising, use student information for targeted advertising, or build commercial profiles of students. Schools control student participation and may request access, correction, export, deletion, or an end to further collection.

1. Operator and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Chi Cheung Lau, doing business as Brainy Bee ("Brainy Bee," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information through the Brainy Bee Schools mobile applications and related school services (the "Service").

This policy applies to the Schools version only. It does not govern the separate Brainy Bee consumer application.

2. School-authorized educational use

Brainy Bee Schools is offered to students only through a participating school or district. The school determines which students and staff may use the Service and the educational purposes for which it is used.

For children under 13 in the United States, a school may authorize collection under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) on a parent's behalf when the Service is used for the benefit of the school, within the educational context, and for no unrelated commercial purpose. Brainy Bee remains responsible for its own compliance with COPPA. Our COPPA Direct Notice gives schools and families additional details.

Where a school relies on the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) school-official exception, Brainy Bee processes education-record information only for the school-authorized service, under the school's direction and contractual control, and does not redisclose that information except as permitted by the agreement and applicable law.

4. How information is collected

  • From the school or Clever: authorized identity, roster, class, grade, and school information.
  • From teachers and authorized staff: assignments, class controls, labels, school information, and optional school branding.
  • From use of the Service: answers, scores, progress, preferences, timestamps, and technical events generated when a user signs in or practices math.
  • From devices and service providers: identifiers, request metadata, local settings, and security or diagnostic information needed to provide the Service.

5. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • authenticate the user and confirm school, role, class, and license access;
  • deliver assignments, math practice, progress, badges, and teacher dashboards;
  • apply school and teacher controls to student features;
  • synchronize authorized information across devices;
  • respond to support, access, correction, export, and deletion requests;
  • protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Service;
  • diagnose errors and improve the reliability and accessibility of the school-requested Service; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce school agreements.

6. No sale, advertising, or unrelated commercial use

We do not sell or rent student personal information. We do not show third-party advertising in Brainy Bee Schools. We do not use student information for targeted advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, marketing to students, commercial profiling, or building a profile for an unrelated commercial purpose.

We do not condition participation on a student providing more personal information than is reasonably necessary for the school-authorized educational activity.

7. When information is disclosed

We disclose information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • To the school: authorized teachers and school personnel may access information for students and classes within their assigned educational responsibilities.
  • To service providers: providers such as Google Firebase and Clever process information to authenticate users, host the Service, synchronize school data, or provide infrastructure. They may process data only to provide contracted services and must protect it appropriately.
  • For legal or safety reasons: when required by valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the Service, users, or others. Where permitted, we will direct requests for education records to the school and notify the school before disclosure.
  • Business transition: only if a successor agrees in writing to the same student-data restrictions, applicable law permits the transfer, and affected schools receive any required notice or choice.

Our current providers and their purposes are listed on the Subprocessor List.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the specific school-authorized purpose. We do not retain children's personal information indefinitely.

  • School account, roster, class, assignment, and progress data is retained while the school's account is active and the information remains needed for current or school-requested historical educational reporting.
  • Following termination or an authorized deletion request, the school normally receives a 30-day export window. Targeted production deletion is completed within 60 days, and residual backup copies expire within 90 days after production deletion, unless law requires a different period.
  • Clever OAuth security state normally expires after five minutes and is deleted after successful redemption.
  • Security and diagnostic records are retained for no more than 12 months unless they are needed longer to investigate a specific incident, prevent abuse, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal requirement.
  • Local device information remains until it is cleared, the app is removed, or the operating system replaces or expires a device backup.

The complete schedule and school offboarding process appear in our Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

9. School, parent, and student rights

Subject to identity and authority verification, the school may:

  • request a description or copy of personal information associated with its students;
  • review and request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request a structured export;
  • request deletion of a student's information;
  • refuse further use or online collection of a student's information; and
  • terminate the school's use of the Service.

Parents, guardians, and eligible students should normally submit education-record requests to their school so the school can verify identity and coordinate all relevant records. They may also contact our Privacy Officer directly. We will verify the request and, when appropriate, coordinate with the school. A parent may request review or deletion of a child under 13's personal information or refuse further collection or use.

Instructions are available in the Data Request Procedure. We will not require more verification information than is reasonably necessary and will not disclose student information to an unverified requester.

10. Security

We maintain a written information security program designed for the sensitivity of student information and the size and scope of the Service. Safeguards include role-based access, Firebase Authentication, encrypted network transport, school and class authorization checks, restricted administrative access, service-provider review, logging, backups, and incident-response procedures.

No security measure can guarantee absolute security. Schools and users should protect credentials, keep devices updated, and promptly report suspected misuse. More information is available in the Security Overview.

11. International and cross-border processing

Brainy Bee is operated from Quebec, Canada, and service providers may process information in the United States, Canada, or other locations described in their service terms. Information may therefore be subject to the laws of those places. Where required, we assess cross-border privacy factors and use written agreements intended to provide appropriate protection.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version with a new effective date and provide schools with notice before a material change takes effect. If a material change requires new authorization or consent, we will obtain it before applying the change to student information.
