Brainy Bee Schools Subprocessor List

Last reviewed: August 2, 2026
Change-notice contact: paulofrancis.software@gmail.com

A Subprocessor is a provider engaged by Brainy Bee to process school or student information in order to provide or secure Brainy Bee Schools. Schools should preserve a copy of this list with their signed Data Privacy Addendum.

Platforms and public-document hosting

The following platforms are not intended to receive Student Data from Brainy Bee, but may receive ordinary platform or web request information:

  • Apple Inc. distributes the iOS app, provides operating-system services, and may maintain user-controlled device backups. Brainy Bee does not receive payment-card data from Apple for the Schools Service.
  • Google LLC / Google Play. distributes the Android app, provides operating-system services, and may maintain user-controlled device backups. The Schools Service does not use Google Play billing.
  • GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft. will host these public legal pages through GitHub Pages. Visitors' IP address and request metadata may be processed when they open the public pages. Student records should never be submitted to GitHub.

Changes

Brainy Bee will evaluate new Subprocessors before allowing them to process Student Data. Under the proposed Data Privacy Addendum, Schools receive at least 30 days' notice before a new provider materially processes Student Data when practicable. Schools may submit a reasonable privacy objection during that period.

Questions or objections may be sent to paulofrancis.software@gmail.com.

Provider locations and contract assurances must be confirmed against the production Firebase and Clever agreements before this list becomes final.
