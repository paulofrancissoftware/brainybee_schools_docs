Platforms and public-document hosting

The following platforms are not intended to receive Student Data from Brainy Bee, but may receive ordinary platform or web request information:

  • Apple Inc. distributes the iOS app and provides operating-system services. Brainy Bee excludes app-managed files from Apple cloud backup and does not receive payment-card data from Apple for the Schools Service.
  • Google LLC / Google Play. distributes the Android app and provides operating-system services. Brainy Bee disables Android app backup and device transfer for the Schools app. The Schools Service does not use Google Play billing.
  • GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft. hosts these public legal pages through GitHub Pages. Visitors' IP address and request metadata may be processed when they open the public pages. Student records should never be submitted to GitHub.

Changes

Brainy Bee will evaluate new Subprocessors before allowing them to process Student Data. Under an executed Data Privacy Addendum, Schools receive at least 30 days' notice before a new provider materially processes Student Data when practicable. Schools may submit a reasonable privacy objection during that period.

Questions or objections may be sent to paulofrancis.software@gmail.com.

Production locations and the applicable Google and Clever privacy and security terms were recorded in the internal Subprocessor Assurance Register and last reviewed on August 15, 2026.
