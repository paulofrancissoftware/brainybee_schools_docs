This notice explains the personal information Brainy Bee Schools collects from students under 13, how it is used and disclosed, and how a school or parent may review it, delete it, or stop further collection. It should be delivered to the school or district decision-maker before student information is collected. Schools may also provide it directly to parents.

2. Why the school receives this notice

Brainy Bee Schools is a school-authorized online educational service. A US school or district may act as a parent's agent and authorize collection of personal information from a child under 13 when the information is used for the benefit of the school and for no unrelated commercial purpose.

Brainy Bee is responsible for complying with COPPA as the operator. This notice does not transfer that responsibility to the school. The school decides whether Brainy Bee Schools is appropriate for its students and whether the person providing school authorization has authority to do so.

3. Information collected

Depending on what Clever and the school make available and which features the school uses, Brainy Bee Schools may collect:

  • student display name, Clever and Firebase identifiers, school or district identifier, role, grade or grade band, class or section membership, and school year;
  • assignment participation, selected math operation and difficulty, answers, question-level reports, scores, percentages, duration, completion timestamps, and progress or achievement badges;
  • avatar, app preferences, active class context, and teacher-configured class controls;
  • device or service identifiers, IP address and request metadata received by providers, sign-in timestamps, OAuth security state, and limited diagnostic or security records; and
  • local device information needed to resume a game, remember preferences, or schedule a local practice reminder.

The Service does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, health information, microphone recordings, camera captures, payment-card information, or advertising identifiers from students. Students do not have a public profile, public chat, or public posting feature.

4. How information is used

Student information is used only to authenticate school users, place students in authorized classes, deliver math practice and assignments, calculate and display progress, apply teacher controls, synchronize school-requested records, support users, secure the Service, and maintain its reliability.

For a competition assignment, enrolled students may see participant ranks and performance information in a class-limited leaderboard. The current student is labeled "You," and classmates appear only under generic labels such as "Competitor 1." Authorized teachers can see student names with results for educational review.

Student information is not sold, rented, used for targeted advertising or marketing, used to build an unrelated commercial profile, or used for an unrelated commercial purpose.

5. Disclosure

Student information is available to authorized school personnel with a legitimate educational need. In competition assignments, enrolled classmates may see ranks and performance information under generic competitor labels; the student leaderboard does not provide classmates' names or direct account identifiers. Information is also processed by service providers needed to operate the Service, currently including Google Firebase for authentication, database, cloud functions, and infrastructure, and Clever for school sign-in and roster information.

Other narrowly scoped technical providers are listed on the Subprocessor List. Brainy Bee requires appropriate confidentiality and security protections before allowing a provider to process children's personal information.

Information is not made publicly available. It may be disclosed when required by valid law or to protect the security and safety of the Service, subject to applicable school notice requirements.

6. Retention

Student information is retained only while reasonably necessary for the school-authorized educational purpose. It is not retained indefinitely. Active school records remain available while the school's account is active and for school-requested historical reporting. Following termination or an authorized deletion request, the schedule provides a 30-day export window and production deletion within 60 days. Inaccessible Firestore point-in-time recovery versions expire within seven days, and no longer-lived managed Firestore backups are enabled. Ordinary service logs are retained for 30 days, while Google-required audit logs are retained for 400 days in a locked log bucket.

See the Data Retention and Deletion Policy for the full schedule.

7. Review, deletion, and stopping collection

The authorizing school, and a verified parent where applicable, may:

  • ask what personal information has been collected from a student;
  • review the student's personal information;
  • request correction or a structured export;
  • request deletion of the student's personal information; and
  • refuse further use or online collection of the student's personal information.

Requests may be sent to paulofrancis.software@gmail.com. Parents should normally begin with the school so it can verify identity and coordinate education records. Brainy Bee will not disclose a student's information until the requester's identity and authority are reasonably verified.

If authorization is withdrawn or deletion makes the account unable to function, the student's access to Brainy Bee Schools may end.

8. School authorization process

Brainy Bee relies on school authorization only after an authorized school or district representative has received this notice and entered into the School Services Agreement and Data Privacy Addendum. Authorization is not obtained through a student or through a child's claim to be a teacher.

The school should make this notice and the Privacy Policy available to parents and should provide any additional parent notice required by school policy or applicable law. A customizable Parent/Guardian Notice Template is available.

9. Material changes

Brainy Bee will notify the school before materially changing the collection, use, or disclosure practices described in this notice. New school authorization or parental consent will be obtained before a change when required by law.
