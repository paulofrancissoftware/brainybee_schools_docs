3. Review procedure

  1. Confirm the service remains necessary and data is limited to its documented purpose.
  2. Review current privacy, security, incident, deletion, subprocessor, and cross-border terms.
  3. Record material changes and assess whether School notice, objection, consent, or a revised PIA is required.
  4. Preserve a dated private copy or reliable record of accepted terms and account acceptance.
  5. Remove or replace a provider that cannot provide protection appropriate to the information and risk.

4. Decision

Google Cloud/Firebase and Clever are approved operationally for the limited pilot configuration described in this register. No optional analytics, advertising, payment, crash-reporting, social, camera, QR-generation, or external font provider is authorized to receive Student Data.
